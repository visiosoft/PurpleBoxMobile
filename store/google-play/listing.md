## Full description (max 4000)

```
PurpleBox is the mobile workspace for the PurpleBox Storage team in Dubai. It connects to the same PurpleBox CRM you already use in the office, so every lead, conversation and unit is up to date wherever you are.

Sign in with your existing PurpleBox CRM account. No separate registration is needed.

LEADS
• See every lead assigned to you, filtered by status, with live counts
• Open a lead to call, WhatsApp or email in one tap
• Update the status, log call and message attempts, and add notes
• Check the AI lead score: the band, the reasoning and the suggested next step
• Convert a qualified lead into a customer

WHATSAPP
• Your WhatsApp Business inbox: Mine, Waiting, Tenants, Unassigned and Quiet
• Reply with text, photos and videos taken with the camera or picked from the gallery
• Play voice notes and open documents customers send
• See how long each customer has been waiting for a reply

FOLLOW-UPS
• A daily list of who to follow up with and when
• Schedule the next follow-up straight from a lead or a chat

SEARCH UNITS
• Look up any storage unit by number, size or status
• Check whether a unit is free for a specific date range
• See the price, dimensions, discounts and recent contracts on a unit while you're on the phone

AI ASSISTANT
• Ask questions about leads, units and contracts in plain English, or speak them aloud
• The assistant proposes actions such as drafting a quote or an email, and nothing runs until you confirm it

FOR MANAGERS
• An overview of the team's pipeline, new leads by day and an AI insight card
• Assign leads and keep track of the lead board

NOTIFICATIONS
• Get notified the moment a new lead is assigned to you

PurpleBox is for PurpleBox Storage staff. You need an active PurpleBox CRM account to sign in.
```
