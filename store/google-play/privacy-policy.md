# PurpleBox Mobile App — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 October 2026. Draft: have it reviewed before publishing.*

This policy explains how the PurpleBox mobile app ("the App"), published by
PurpleBox Storage, Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("PurpleBox", "we"), handles
information. The App is a work tool for PurpleBox staff. It is the mobile
client of the PurpleBox CRM, and only people with a PurpleBox CRM account
issued by the company can sign in.

## Information the App handles

- **Account information.** Your name, work email address and role, used to
  sign you in and show the right workspace. Your session token is kept in
  the device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
- **Business records.** Leads, customer names and phone numbers, WhatsApp
  conversations, notes, follow-ups, units and contracts that you view or
  update. These records belong to PurpleBox and are stored on PurpleBox's
  servers, not on your device, apart from a temporary cache.
- **Photos and videos you choose to attach.** Only when you pick or take a
  photo or video to send in a WhatsApp reply. The App never reads your
  gallery in the background.
- **Microphone (voice commands).** When you tap the microphone, speech is
  turned into text by your device's speech-recognition service. The App
  receives only the text, and does not record or store audio.
- **Push-notification token.** A device token so we can notify you about
  newly assigned leads.

The App does not collect your location or your phone's contacts, and it
contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## How we use it

Only to run the App's features: signing you in, showing and updating CRM
records, sending WhatsApp messages on PurpleBox's behalf, answering
assistant questions, and sending work notifications. We do not sell
information or use it for advertising.

## Sharing

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to PurpleBox's own
servers. It is shared only with service providers that run those services
for us, such as hosting, the WhatsApp Business Platform for customer
messages, push-notification delivery, and AI processing for the assistant
and lead scoring. Each provider acts on our instructions. We may also
disclose information where the law requires it.

## Retention and deletion

Business records are kept according to PurpleBox's record-keeping
obligations. Signing out removes your session from the device. To have your
account or personal information deleted, contact the address below. Accounts
are managed by the company, so access ends when your employment ends.

## Children

The App is for PurpleBox employees and is not directed at anyone under 18.
